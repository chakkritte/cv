About Me

I am an Assistant Professor and Assistant Director for Digital Innovation at SGtech, Naresuan University. My research interests lie at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Smart Grids.

I specialize in Neural Architecture Search (NAS), Computer Vision, and their applications in renewable energy and sustainability.
